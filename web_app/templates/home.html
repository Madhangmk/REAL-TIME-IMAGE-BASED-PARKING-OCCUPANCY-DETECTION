Project Overview

Smart Parking Space Detection is a cutting-edge computer vision application designed to automate the process of monitoring parking lot occupancy.

By leveraging advanced deep learning algorithms and the YOLO (You Only Look Once) model, our system identifies vehicles in real-time and analyzes predefined parking zones to provide instant updates on space availability.
